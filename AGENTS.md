## Importing past-paper PDFs

When the user supplies exam-paper PDFs or requests question-bank imports, read
and follow [docs/PAST_PAPER_IMPORT_RULES.md](docs/PAST_PAPER_IMPORT_RULES.md) before
extracting or changing question data. These rules also apply when repairing a
previous import. Preserve the existing study-page design and unrelated content.
